# Code of conduct

Hopper follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults and spam aren't allowed, here, in issues, PRs or on the Discord.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainers on [Discord](https://discord.gg/kKqTbptz2T).
Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs, or block people who break these rules.
